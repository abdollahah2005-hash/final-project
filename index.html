Happy birthday, Somy

I wrote you something. Light the candle first.

Somy,

I know I’m probably the last person you expect a message from, but I couldn’t let today pass without telling you what’s inside me.

I’m sorry. For every time I hurt you, for every time I made you feel pain, even the times I didn’t realize it then.

I’m not writing this to sound poetic. I’m writing it because I’ve replayed those moments more times than I can count, and every single time I wish I could step back into them and choose differently.

Every time you crossed my mind, I wanted to come closer. And every time, I did the opposite and pulled away, thinking that was the right thing. It wasn’t. The right thing was to be next to you.

I told myself I was protecting us. The truth is I was scared, and I was wrong, and you were the one who paid for it. I regret that more than I know how to put into words.

I’m sorry for every time I turned down your attempts to bring us back to how we were, when we played Roblox and Minecraft together.

You reached out, and I let the door close. You deserved someone who stayed, who said yes to those old nights, who said “I miss it too.” I did miss it. I just never said so.

You’re the only girl I’ve ever truly loved, with every part of my heart. It was my stupid decisions that pushed us apart. Not you. Never you.

If I could do the impossible to make everything right again, I would.

And if you ever give me the chance, I won’t only say that I’ve changed. I’ll show you, slowly and patiently, in small things, on your terms. My heart and my mind are yours: one to love you honestly, the other to never repeat the mistakes that cost me you.

March 13, 2025

That’s the day everything began. It’s the day you met the person you first fell for, and I promise you I’ll become that person again. The old Abdullah: the one who was kind, who laughed with you, who showed up. I don’t want to turn back time. I want to bring back the best of me, the part I let slip away.

And I promise to be a man in every word I say to you. If I say I’ll be there, I’ll be there. If I say I’m sorry, I’ll prove it. No more running, no more silence, no more decisions made out of fear.

I’m sorry, my love. I’m sorry, Somy.

Every word on this page is true. I didn’t write it to win you back or to make you feel anything you don’t. I wrote it because you deserve to hear the truth from me, finally, without excuses.

I’m not expecting a reply today, or anything at all. I just want you to know you deserve every good thing, and that today is a good day because of you.

Happy birthday. May this year be the kindest one yet.
